MERIDIAN FINANCIAL GROUP — Q4 2024 EARNINGS REPORT
Released: January 28, 2025
Prepared by: Office of the CFO

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Meridian Financial Group delivered record results in Q4 2024, driven by strong
loan origination volumes, disciplined cost management, and continued expansion
in our wealth management division. Total revenue reached $3.45B for the quarter,
representing 11.3% growth over Q3 2024 and 38% year-over-year growth.

Net income of $850M exceeded analyst consensus of $780M, reflecting a net margin
of 24.6% — our highest quarterly margin since inception. This improvement was
primarily driven by operating leverage as revenue scaled faster than headcount
growth (12% vs 65 FTEs added in Q4).

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

- Revenue: $3.45M quarterly, $11.8M full-year 2024
- Net Income: $850K quarterly, $2.4M full-year 2024
- Customer base expanded to 1,750 (+15.1% QoQ)
- Gross margin improved to 46.4%, up from 43.5% in Q3
- MRR reached $165K in December, representing $1.98M ARR
- LTV/CAC ratio improved to 15.9x, up from 12.9x in November
- Churn rate declined to 1.9%, lowest in company history

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE

North America remains our largest market at $2.07M in Q4 revenue (60% of total),
growing 11.3% quarter-over-quarter. EMEA contributed $897K (26%) with strong
traction in the UK and Germany. APAC delivered $483K (14%) with accelerating
growth in Singapore and Australia.

FORWARD GUIDANCE

Management is guiding for Q1 2025 revenue of $3.8-4.0M, representing 10-16%
sequential growth. We expect continued margin expansion as our platform scales.
Key investments planned for 2025 include:
- Expansion into Latin American markets (target: Q2 2025 launch)
- AI-powered risk analytics platform (beta: Q3 2025)
- Regulatory technology suite for Basel IV compliance

RISK FACTORS

- Rising interest rate environment may pressure loan demand in H2 2025
- Regulatory uncertainty around Basel IV implementation timelines
- Competitive pressure from fintech entrants in the SMB lending segment
- FX headwinds expected to impact EMEA margins by 50-80bps
